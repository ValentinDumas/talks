## 📖 Overview

When your next hit is co-composed by an AI — this talk explores creative AI at the intersection of music and technology. From text-to-music generation to audio mastering and stem separation, the session includes live demos and original AI-composed tracks. A unique blend of tech and art for a DevFest audience.
